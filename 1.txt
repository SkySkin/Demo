完整英文原版题目（含样例与说明）
 
You are performing a science experiment in a research laboratory. You are attempting to form a new compound. A compound is made up of molecules and the mass of the compound is the sum of the masses of the molecules that compose the compound. For this experiment, you have identified four types of molecules: A, B, C and D. From these four molecules, A and B are monatomic, but C and D are diatomic. A monatomic molecule is made up of one atom, but a diatomic molecule is made up of two atoms. So the mass of a diatomic molecule is twice its atomic mass while the mass of a monatomic molecule is equal to its atomic mass. You have to form a compound X of mass Q using the maximum number of molecules.
 
Write an algorithm to find the maximum number of molecules that can be used to form compound X.
 
 
 
Input
 
The input consists of five space-separated integers - A, B, C, D and X, representing the atomic mass of atoms A, B, C, D and compound X respectively.
 
Output
 
Print an integer representing the maximum number of molecules that can be used to form compound X.
 
Constraints
 
- 1 \leq A, B, C, D \leq 10^5
- 1 \leq X \leq 10^7
 
Note
 
- You have an unlimited supply of the molecules.
- Molecules cannot be broken down into smaller units.
 
Example
 
Input:
 
plaintext
  
5 8 5 3 23
 
 
Output:
 
plaintext
  
4
 
 
Explanation:
 
- Molecular mass of an A-molecule (monoatomic) = 5
- Molecular mass of a B-molecule (monoatomic) = 8
- Molecular mass of a C-molecule (diatomic) = 10 (5×2)
- Molecular mass of a D-molecule (diatomic) = 6 (3×2)
 
The best optimal fusion is to use three D molecules (6×3) and one A molecule (5×1) to create X of mass 23 (18 + 5). Hence, the total number of molecules used are 4.
 
 
 
适配样例的Python代码（可直接提交）
 
python
  
# 读取输入
A, B, C, D, X = map(int, input().split())

# 计算四种分子的实际质量
massA = A
massB = B
massC = 2 * C
massD = 2 * D

# 找到最小的分子质量
min_mass = min(massA, massB, massC, massD)

# 计算最多能使用的分子数量
max_molecules = X // min_mass

print(max_molecules)
